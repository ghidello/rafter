What we are reviewing

  • Fixed indentation, separate descriptions, and direct dependencies such as [test] <-- (build) follow the agreed direction. The three help layouts and Continue/caught-failure reporting are accepted for now. Process diagnostics share the compact layout from scenario 18. Multiple-entry headings and summaries preserve requested order.
  • The flatter layout is the selected direction. It moves target descriptions to the left margin and uses a two-space content gutter. Original child/script indentation and exception stack indentation remain intact. Help and input-validation layouts are unchanged.
  • Combined output shows one readable ordering. Independent stdout and stderr do not guarantee that ordering.
  • Every transcript, timing, path, SDK version, and exception here is synthetic. These are design fixtures, not evidence that Rafter or a particular .NET version has been implemented or tested.
  • Child lines keep their original whitespace; long literal lines scroll. Rafter descriptions and diagnostics wrap. Narrow help wrapping here is illustrative, not a completed terminal wrapping algorithm.
