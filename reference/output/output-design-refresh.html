Review in progress: compact target sections

The reviewed design uses the entry target to frame the invocation. Dependency steps have [target] headings and their own durations. An entry with a body gets its own execution section after dependencies; a dependency-only entry needs no repeated section. Its single completion is the final summary. Child-output prefix routing remains open.

The preferred fixed-gutter design uses column zero for target sections and final results, two spaces for process/cleanup/error headings and cleanup results, and four spaces for content. Diagnostics and exception details align at four spaces instead of becoming deeper for each phase. Native stack and child-output indentation is retained. Quiet keeps standalone diagnostics; plain/redirected authored and child output still follows the prefix selector.

Command cleanup names the script and selected entry, matching the invocation header: Command cleanup | probe.cs | finish. Its result repeats that ownership, including in quiet mode, where the heading is hidden.

  • Prefix script-authored results with four spaces and |. Child-process presentation uses four spaces and >, preserving existing indentation and stream routing.
  • Keep one settled outcome for every target. The entry completion is integrated into the final summary, after body, processes, target cleanup, and command cleanup settle.
  • Successful cleanup retains its authored messages. Verbose output adds its description, result, and duration.
  • Keep failures and failed cleanup explicit in every mode. Callback exception details remain complete; process details follow verbose policy.
  • Normal mode has no timing tuples. Dependency step durations include their own work and target cleanup; the final entry result shows whole-invocation elapsed time.
  • Other grouping targets retain compact (group) results. Verbose names dependency, body, and cleanup components; shared dependencies already completed contribute no additional time.
  • Verbose traversal totals can overlap. Whole-invocation elapsed time also includes command cleanup and overhead. Timing components and rounded totals here are illustrative, not new measurements.
  • Verbose shows the complete representative executable and arguments plus working directory. Runtime presentation must quote arguments appropriately and redact sensitive values.

This is a design comparison, not a new execution mode or a terminal emulator. Combined ordering is illustrative; independently redirected streams cannot establish their shared-terminal order.
